01 · About

Two disciplines, one desk

A Computer Engineering graduate (Honours, AI & ML) who's spent the last two years moving between applied machine learning and full-stack engineering — and stopped seeing them as separate tracks.

Four internships have taken me through data cleaning at PCI LLP, live Django codebases at BitSpark, a full MERN build at Edunet, and — most recently — writing the report-generation engine for a cybersecurity firm's internal pen-testing platform. Different stacks, same lesson: a model's accuracy matters less than whether the report it produces actually gets read by a doctor, an admin, or a security analyst.

RasaCare, an Ayurvedic diagnosis platform, is the clearest example — an XGBoost classifier and a 23,000-node graph database, wrapped in an interface a clinician can actually use without a manual. I care about the same thing whether I'm training a model or writing a REST endpoint: does it hold up outside the demo.
